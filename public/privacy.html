Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 August 2026

This policy explains what personal information Dates.care collects, why we collect it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the choices you have. It applies to the Dates.care website and web app at dates.care and to every account created on them.

Dates.care is an online dating service operated from Ontario, Canada. We handle personal information in line with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA). If you are outside Canada, your information is processed in Canada and the United States under this policy.

You must be 18 or older to use Dates.care. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn that we have, we delete the account and its data.

1.3 Information from third parties

If you sign in with Google, Facebook or Apple, that provider sends us a limited set of information so we can create or open your account. From Google Sign-In we receive:

  • your name and profile picture,
  • your primary Google Account email address, and
  • a stable identifier that lets us recognise you on your next sign-in.

Google user data - limited use. We use the information received through Google Sign-In only to create and authenticate your Dates.care account and to prefill your name, email and profile picture. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it, we do not transfer it to anyone except as described in section 4, and we do not allow humans to read it except with your consent, for security or legal reasons, or where it has been aggregated and anonymised. We do not request access to your Gmail, Drive, Contacts, Calendar or any other Google service. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. You can revoke our access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

We do not receive your card or bank details from our payment processor - only whether a payment succeeded, the amount, and a reference for the transaction.

3. Automated processing

Photos and messages pass through automated moderation that looks for nudity, violence, scams and abuse. A match can hide content or suspend an account. No automated decision permanently deletes an account on its own - a person reviews suspensions, and you can contest one by writing to support@dates.care.

If Dates.care is ever sold or merged, your information may transfer to the buyer; we will tell you before that happens and this policy continues to apply until you are told otherwise.

5. What other members can see

Your profile - your name, photos, age, location and anything you write on it - is visible to other signed-in members. Your email address, phone number, date of birth and any verification documents are never shown to other members. Anything you send in a message is visible to the person you sent it to, and may be seen by our moderators if it is reported.

6. Where your information is held

Your information is stored on servers in Canada and the United States. Where information is transferred outside Canada, it remains subject to this policy and to contractual protections with our providers, and it may be accessible to authorities in those countries under their laws.

7. How long we keep it

  • Active accounts - for as long as your account exists.
  • Deleted accounts - profile and content are removed within 30 days.
  • Verification documents - deleted within 90 days of the check completing.
  • Payment records - kept for seven years, as tax and accounting law requires.
  • Safety records - where an account was suspended for abuse or fraud, we keep a minimal record so the same person cannot simply return.

8. How we protect it

  • All traffic is encrypted in transit with TLS, and data is encrypted at rest.
  • Passwords are stored only as salted hashes and are never readable by us.
  • Database access is restricted by row-level security so members can reach only their own data.
  • Staff access is limited to those who need it, and is logged.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach occurs that creates a real risk of significant harm, we will notify you and the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada as PIPEDA requires.

9. Your rights

  • Access - ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction - have inaccurate information fixed.
  • Deletion - delete your account and its data, in your account settings or by asking us.
  • Portability - receive your data in a common machine-readable format.
  • Withdraw consent - for anything you consented to, including marketing and, where you signed in with Google, by revoking access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Complain - to us first, and then to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada at priv.gc.ca.

Write to privacy@dates.care and we will respond within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm your identity first.

10. Cookies and similar technologies

We use only what the service needs to function: a session cookie or local storage entry that keeps you signed in, and a preference store for settings such as theme. We do not use advertising cookies and we do not run third-party ad trackers. Clearing your browser storage signs you out.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top and, where the change is significant, tell you in the app or by email at least 21 days before it takes effect.
